Code Compass

Year 2025
Role Branding · Visual Identity
Type School · Workshop
Tools Illustrator · Figma · p5.js

Overview

A school assignment built around Bernard Lievegoed College in Maastricht, where we had to design and run a creative coding workshop for first-year secondary school students and wrap the whole thing in a full brand. We chose to frame ourselves as a small studio built around teaching, which meant the identity had to feel playful enough for the students while still looking serious enough for the teachers actually booking the workshop.

Most of the work went into research and visual direction long before anything landed on the artboard, moving through moodboards, colour exploration and typography trials until everything came together in a single brand board that shaped both the website and the printed workshop material. Ilja and i led the design side, and the whole thing ended with us teaching the workshop live in a classroom, where every student walked out with their own digital character they had coded themselves.

Deliverables
  • Brand identity
  • Website design
  • Workshop booklet
  • Participation certificate
  • Live workshop

The takeaways

a booklet to work in during the session, and a certificate for every student who made it to the end of the workshop.
