Hi, I'm Louis.

I'm a designer from Maastricht who grew up just across the border in Belgium and now lives in Marbella, where I'm interning at Manifesto Design. I like work that looks simple because someone took the time to get it right, which is why I ask a lot of questions before I start and keep refining long after most people would call it done.

Alongside design I'm fascinated by AI and what it makes possible, so I use it every day and build small tools of my own to see how far it can go. When I'm not behind a screen you'll find me training, because sport is what keeps my head clear, or somewhere in the sun with good food and a game on.

How I work

Three things I hold myself to on every project.

  1. Research first.

    I keep asking questions until I understand the problem. Only then do I open Figma.

  2. Details to the end.

    Spacing, type and colour get the same attention as the big idea.

  3. Quality over quantity.

    I would rather show three finished things than ten that are almost there.
